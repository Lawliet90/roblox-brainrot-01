**Steal a Sigma — Ohio Brainrot Heist**: a 3D Roblox brainrot game. Meme brainrots walk a neon conveyor through the arena — buy them with Rizz, park them on your base plot and watch the money tick up every second. Climb the rarity ladder Common → Rare → Epic → Legendary → Mythic → Secret, roll mutations (Golden x2, Diamond x5, Rainbow x10, Glitched x25), rebirth for a permanent income multiplier, and slap-and-steal from other players' bases (or buy a lock to keep yours safe). Random events keep everyone running: Fanum Tax (hide in your base or lose 10%), Ohio Storm (tornado + meteors, dash home or ragdoll), and Skibidi Boss raids that drop rare brainrots. Original cast: Toilet Skibidi, Ohio Rizzler, Mewing Cat, Sigma Wolf, Fanum Tax Goblin, Grimace Shake, Skibidi Boss, Mewing Golem, Ohio Final Boss, Gigachad Prime and The Rizzler.

## Run it

```sh
rojo build default.project.json --output game.rbxl && mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

Open `game.rbxl` in Roblox Studio to play it: spawn in the plaza, walk to the conveyor, press **E** (or click BUY) when a brainrot walks past, and it earns Rizz on your base. **I** opens the brainrot index, click another player to slap them, **E** near an enemy base steals their brainrot — carry it home before the timer runs out.
